# House → Play School & Teacher Training Centre — design concept

This is a design-only makeover of the house in the walkthrough video. **No structural work** is involved: no walls
moved or added, no new openings, no plumbing changes. Everything shown is paint, murals, flooring finishes, furniture,
lighting fixtures, signage and décor, so the building stays exactly as it is.

**Watch:** [`video/little-sprouts-redesign-walkthrough.mp4`](video/little-sprouts-redesign-walkthrough.mp4)
(1080×1920 vertical, about 1 min 38 s, works well on a phone or WhatsApp).

The video follows the same route as the original walkthrough: the outside, the front door, the main hall, the arch,
the back room, the kitchen and the store. Each space shows the real footage first, then a **before → after** wipe on
the exact same frame, with pins marking where each item goes. It ends with a floor-by-floor plan.

> "Little Sprouts" is a **placeholder name**. Change `SCHOOL = ...` in `source/stage/sc_ext.py` and
> `source/video/make_frags.py`, then re-run, to put your own name on the signboard and in the titles.

## 1. Floor-by-floor zoning

| Floor | Use | Spaces |
|---|---|---|
| **Terrace** (green shade already there) | Outdoor play | Artificial grass, sand & water play, gardening corner, safety netting on the parapet |
| **2nd floor** | **Teacher Training Centre** | Training hall (projector, 15–20 seats), demo/practice classroom, resource library, office & counselling |
| **1st floor** | Classrooms | Nursery, LKG, UKG, nap & quiet room, kids' toilets |
| **Ground floor** (filmed) | Play & welcome | Reception, play hall (doubles as a training hall after 2 PM), classroom 1, snack & hygiene station, resource store |

The upper floors weren't filmed (only their balconies show from the street), so their layout is a **proposal**. The
facade banners in the video advertise them: *Play School · Classrooms on this floor* (1st) and *Teacher Training Centre*
(2nd).

**If only the ground floor is available**, everything still works. The play hall becomes the teacher-training room from
2 PM to 5 PM: foam mats stay, the slide and ball pool sit in the corner, a pull-down screen drops over the mural and
8–16 stackable chairs come out (see the *Same hall · teacher training* scene).

## 4. Child-safety checklist (cheap, no structural work)

- Corner guards on wall edges and furniture; socket covers on every low socket
- Anti-skid flooring: foam mats in the play zone, vinyl or anti-skid tiles in walkways
- Soft flooring under the slide and around the ball pool
- Door finger-guards and slow door closers; latch on the main gate placed out of children's reach
- Fire extinguisher, smoke detector and first-aid kit (in the store); emergency numbers at reception
- CCTV at the entrance, hall and classrooms; visitor register at reception
- Safety gate at the bottom and top of the stairs; safety netting on the balconies and terrace
- Child-height hand-wash and step stools; drinking water within reach
- Before opening, check your local preschool registration and fire-safety requirements

## 6. How the video was made

1. **Analysed the walkthrough.** Picked the sharpest frame of each space and brightened the dark rooms.
2. **Solved the camera for each frame.** Used vanishing lines and known shapes (doors, the arch, room corners) to
   recover lens and position, about 1–3 px error. Every hall view is calibrated in one shared room model, so the
   design stays consistent between shots.
3. **Virtual staging on your real frames.**
   - **Walls and ceilings:** repainted while keeping the real light and shadow; stains and clutter smoothed out.
   - **Murals and floor mats:** perspective-warped onto the actual wall and floor planes.
   - **Furniture:** built and rendered in 3D (three.js) with the matched camera and soft shadows.
   - **Foreground:** objects like door jambs are masked so nothing is painted over them.
4. **Edited with the video-editing skill system (HyperFrames).**
   - **Structure:** a scene-based edit (EDL + time-map) alternating real footage with before/after reveals.
   - **Overlays:** callout pins placed from the 3D positions; a floor-by-floor plan and an outro.
   - **Sound:** a synthesised, license-free music bed with whoosh/pop effects, mastered to −14 LUFS.

These are **concept visualisations, not construction drawings**. Treat sizes as estimates and measure on site before
buying furniture.
