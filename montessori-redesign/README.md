# House → Montessori School & Teacher Training Centre — design concept

This redesigns the whole building, inside and out, as a **Montessori school (toddlers + Children's House) and a
Montessori teacher training centre**. It is **design-only, with no structural changes**: no walls moved, no new
openings, no plumbing changes. Everything is paint, wood finishes, flooring, child-sized furniture, Montessori
materials, lighting, plants and signage.

**Watch:** [`video/little-sprouts-montessori-walkthrough.mp4`](video/little-sprouts-montessori-walkthrough.mp4)
(1080×1920 vertical, about 1 min 38 s). The video follows the route of your original walkthrough. For each space you
see your real footage first, then a **before → after** wipe on the same frame, with pins marking where each item goes.
It ends with a floor-by-floor plan.

## 1. The Montessori approach behind every choice

| Montessori principle | What it looks like in this building |
|---|---|
| **Prepared environment** | Each room is laid out in curriculum areas with everything on **low open shelves**, one activity per tray, always in the same place |
| **Independence** ("Help me to do it myself") | Shoe bench and **child-height hooks** at the entrance; child-height sinks; a learning tower in the kitchen; a self-serve snack table |
| **Calm and beauty** | Warm-white walls, **oak wainscot and oak floors**, sage and terracotta accents, rattan lights, plants. No cartoon murals, no plastic primary colours |
| **Real, child-sized materials** | Wooden furniture sized for children; real glass and ceramic on practical-life trays; classic Montessori materials |
| **Freedom of movement** | Open floor with **work rugs** (rolled in a basket), tables for one or two (not rows), and a **walking-on-the-line ellipse** |
| **Nature** | Plants in every room, nature prints at child eye level, a vertical garden at the entrance, a gardening terrace |
| **Mixed ages and observation** | 3–6 mixed-age Children's House; trainees observe real classes in the morning and practise with the same materials after 2 PM |

## 2. Floor-by-floor zoning

| Floor | Use | Spaces |
|---|---|---|
| **Terrace** (existing green shade) | **Outdoor environment** | Vegetable beds, sand & water, nature table, outdoor practical life (washing, sweeping, watering), safety netting |
| **2nd floor** | **Montessori Teacher Training Centre** | Lecture room, **material practice lab** with a full set of materials, album-writing & observation room, library & office |
| **1st floor** | **Children's House 2** | Second mixed-age 3–6 classroom, rest room, child-sized toilets, art & music corner |
| **Ground floor** (filmed) | **Welcome · Children's House 1 · Toddlers** | Welcome & transition area, Children's House 1 (doubles as the training practicum after 2 PM), toddler community, practical-life kitchen, materials room |

The upper floors weren't in the walkthrough, so their layout is a **proposal**. The balcony banners in the video
signpost them: *Montessori Children's House* on the 1st floor and *Montessori Teacher Training* on the 2nd.

**If only the ground floor is available**, the plan still works. Children's House 1 is the classroom in the morning
and becomes the teacher-training practicum from 2 PM: trainees sit around a demonstration rug, use the same shelves
of materials, and a pull-down screen covers the wall for lectures.

## 4. Teacher training programme (how the spaces are used)

| Time | Children | Trainees |
|---|---|---|
| 9 AM – 1 PM | Toddler community + Children's House 1 (and 2 upstairs) | **Observation** from a quiet chair in the classroom corner (logged in the album & observation room) |
| 2 PM – 5 PM | — | **Practicum** in Children's House 1: lecture on the pull-down screen, then demonstrations on rugs and practice with the real materials |
| Evenings & weekends | — | **Lectures and album writing** in the 2nd-floor training centre; workshops for parents |

Use **low-VOC washable paint**, rounded-edge solid wood or birch-ply furniture, and non-toxic finishes.

## 6. Montessori materials shopping list (starter set per Children's House)

- **Practical life:** child-sized trays, small glass jugs, bowls, spoons, tongs; dressing frames (buttons, zips, bows); child-size broom, dustpan, cloths, apron; watering can and plant mister.
- **Sensorial:** pink tower, brown stair, red rods, knobbed cylinders (4 blocks), colour tablets (boxes 1–3), geometric cabinet, sound cylinders.
- **Language:** sandpaper letters, large moveable alphabet, object baskets, front-facing book display.
- **Mathematics:** number rods, sandpaper numerals, spindle box, golden beads, bead chains, teen and ten boards.
- **Cultural:** sandpaper globe, coloured continent globe, world and continent puzzle maps, botany puzzles, nature table.
- **Toddler community:** low mirror with pull-up bar, stacking rings, object permanence box, knob puzzles, posting boxes, weaning table and chairs, floor bed or cushions.
- **Furniture:** low open shelves (60–90 cm), tables for 1–2 children, child chairs (seat height 26–35 cm), work rugs + rug basket, shoe bench, child-height hooks, learning tower.
- **Training centre:** full material set for the practice lab, projector and pull-down screen, adult chairs, album shelves, observation chairs.

## 7. Child-safety checklist

Corner guards and socket covers · anti-slip finish on oak floors · furniture anchored to walls (shelves, mirror bar) ·
safety glass or acrylic for low mirrors · finger guards and slow closers on doors · gate latch out of children's reach ·
stair gates top and bottom · balcony and terrace netting · fire extinguisher, smoke detector and first-aid kit (materials
room) · CCTV and a visitor register at the welcome desk · check your local preschool registration and fire-safety
requirements before opening.

## 8. How it was made

These are the same real frames and camera solves as the play-school concept, now with a Montessori design layer:

- **Walls and floors:** walls repainted while keeping the real light; oak wainscot, floors, nature prints, the continent map and the quote perspective-warped onto the actual surfaces.
- **Furniture and materials:** a new 3D library of Montessori furniture and materials (three.js) rendered with the matched cameras and soft shadows.
- **Edit:** done with the video-editing skill system (HyperFrames). It has a calm cream/serif theme, a gentle synthesised (license-free) music bed, a review gate, and mastering to −14 LUFS.

These are **concept visualisations, not construction drawings**. Measure on site before ordering furniture.
